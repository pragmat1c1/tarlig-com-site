Privacy

This static website uses no cookies, no advertising or tracking pixels, and no embedded third-party widgets. For anonymous reach measurement it uses Cloudflare Web Analytics (see below).

Reach measurement (Cloudflare Web Analytics)

To measure page views I use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-friendly analytics service provided by Cloudflare, Inc. Measurement is cookieless and without cross-site recognition. No personal profiles are built and no data that could identify individual visitors is stored; only aggregated metrics are collected, such as pages viewed, referring sources (referrers), and approximate geographic origin. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in data-minimizing reach measurement (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). For more information, see Cloudflare's privacy policy at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.
